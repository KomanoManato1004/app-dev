# My Hero Academia

**Genre:** Action, Superhero, Anime
**Creator:** Kōhei Horikoshi
**Platform:** *Crunchyroll, Netflix*

## Why I Love It

- Unique take on superhero society  
- Deep character development  
- Intense battles with emotional stakes  
- Inspiring themes of perseverance and justice

---

## Favorite Characters

3. Izuku Midoriya
